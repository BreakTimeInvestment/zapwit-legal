Zapwit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

Zapwit is a quiz game operated by BreakTimeInvestments. Questions about this policy can be sent to zapwittrivia@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID, created automatically when you open the app. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Game data, such as your progress, scores, levels and settings.
  • Device and usage data, such as device model, operating system, app version, crash reports, approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address) and how you use the app.
  • Advertising data, such as your device's advertising ID, collected by our ad provider when ads are shown.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress.
  • To understand how the game is used and to fix bugs and improve it.
  • To show ads, which keep Zapwit free.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

4. Ads

Zapwit shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID and similar data to show ads and measure their performance. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Services we use

We use Google Firebase (for anonymous sign-in, data storage and analytics) and Google AdMob (for ads). These providers process data on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before using your data for personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings.

Where required by law, our legal basis for processing is your consent (ads and analytics) or our legitimate interest in running and securing the game.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email zapwittrivia@gmail.com. Because accounts are anonymous, we may ask you for your app's account ID (shown in Settings, if available) so we can find your data.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep game data for as long as your anonymous account is active. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing us, and we will do so within a reasonable time, unless the law requires us to keep it. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device.

9. Children

Zapwit is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has used the app, contact zapwittrivia@gmail.com and we will delete the data.

10. International transfers and security

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email zapwittrivia@gmail.com.
